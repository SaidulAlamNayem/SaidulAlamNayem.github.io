The product

How the retail-execution platform works

Used by Unilever Bangladesh (M-Lens) and British American Tobacco Bangladesh (Market Lenz). A field rep photographs the shelf; the system does the rest.

  1. CaptureRep takes shelf photos on a light mobile app, geo-tagged at the outlet
  2. RecogniseComputer vision and OCR read every SKU, sachet and display item
  3. ScoreEach outlet is scored against the brand's execution standards
  4. ActManagers see gaps by outlet, region and channel on web dashboards

Share of shelf

Brand and SKU facings against competitors on every shelf.

Share of sachet

Mini-packs and sachets detected on hangers and strips.

Share of visibility

Posters, danglers and display units checked for presence and placement.

Planogram adherence

Actual shelf layout compared with the agreed planogram.

Also tracked: product availability, out-of-stock flags, visit compliance and field-team scoring. Recognition accuracy is 95%+.
